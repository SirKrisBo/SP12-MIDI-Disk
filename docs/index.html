Help

SP-12 MIDI Disk, page version 1.5 (2026-11-15). The page is a disk drive for the SP-12's MIDI medium: the SP-12 saves its sounds or sequences here and loads them back, over MIDI. It keeps two disks, as you would keep a sound floppy and a sequence floppy: the SOUND disk and the SEQ disk. SAVE each to a file to keep it.

Cables

  • SP-12 MIDI OUT to your MIDI interface's IN.
  • The interface's OUT to SP-12 MIDI IN.

On this page

  1. Press CONNECT. When the browser asks, allow MIDI devices, including control and reprogram (that is SysEx; the SP-12 needs it). If you said no by mistake: click the icon left of the address, allow MIDI, reload the page.
  2. Choose MIDI In (the port the SP-12's MIDI OUT goes to) and MIDI Out. The page remembers them.
  3. The screen says Waiting for SP12.
  4. Press SOUND or SEQ: the disk whose lamp is lit is the one shown, and the one the SP-12 reads.

On the SP-12

Press CASSETTE/DISK, then 3, then the function's number: 1 Save Sequences, 2 Save Sounds, 3 Load Sequences, 4 Load Segment #, 5 Load Sounds, 6 Load Sound #, 7 Verify Sequences, 8 Verify Sounds, 9 Catalog, 0 Format. At "Insert Disk / and Press Enter" press ENTER. A full 20 s of sounds takes about 5 minutes each way.

If the SP-12 stays on "Reading the Disk" or "Writing the Disk" and nothing moves here for 10 seconds, hold NO (7) until it shows "Aborted", then open LOG and download the log.

Two disks

A save from the SP-12 goes to the disk of its kind (Save Sounds to the SOUND disk, Save Sequences to the SEQ disk), and the page then shows that disk; the other disk is not touched. NEW, SAVE and CATALOG work on the disk shown; LOAD puts a file into the disk of its kind.

Load, Verify, Catalog and Format use the disk shown: the SP-12 does not say what it wants until it has read the disk's first block. While it sends, the screen names the disk (Sending SOUNDS or Sending SEQ disk). Sounds loaded with the SEQ disk shown: the screen says SP12 wants SOUND / Press SOUND key; press SOUND and start the function again. Sequences loaded with the SOUND disk shown: the SP-12 shows "Wrong File Type", the screen Disk Info Sent / Wrong file? SEQ; press SEQ and try again.

Files

SAVE writes the disk shown as a .sp12 file: Chrome and Edge on a computer ask where (folder and name; Cancel saves nothing), other browsers download it. LOAD opens .sp12 files (and the test page's .sp12disk), and also the SP12 app's .SPLB12 (sounds) and .SPLQ12 (sequences) and the SP12 tool's .SP12; it tells them apart by their content and puts each into the disk of its kind. A disk opened from an app or tool file is saved as .sp12 too.

The SAVE lamp lights while the disk shown holds data that is not in a file yet. The disks also stay in this browser (after a reload too), but browsers can clear what they keep: save to files. Nothing is sent anywhere: the page talks only to your MIDI ports.

Playing

In the CATALOG of a sound disk, tap a pad to play it on its output (Ch): a pad on an output that is playing cuts that sound, different outputs play together. Greyed pads have data that are not all on the disk. The computer keys play the pads too, as they lie: 1-8 bank D, Q-I bank C, A-K bank B, Z-, bank A (by their place, on any keyboard layout).

RUN / STOP (the big dark key; its lamp is lit while anything plays): runs the segment or song chosen in the SEQ disk's CATALOG (from the sound catalog too: the last one chosen); pressed while anything plays, it stops everything, pads too. Closing the catalog stops too; SOUND / SEQ do not. The sounds come from the SOUND disk. A segment repeats until stopped, as on the SP-12; a song plays to its End.

Tempo: segments play at the page's tempo, 90.0 at the start: TEMPO - / + (a press: 1.0; held: 0.1 at a time), TAP in time (a few taps; a 2 s pause starts again), or tap the screen to type it. A song plays at its own tempo and tempo steps.

The page plays like the SP-12's voices (level, tuning, decay, loops) at 26,042 Hz, without its output filters and analog stage: the SP-12 sounds different in tone.

Sounds

  • Tap a pad: it plays and is chosen (outlined). DECAY: the chosen sound to decay mode or back to tune mode, as the SP-12's Set-up 18 does (lamp lit: decay mode).
  • CH: the chosen sound to the next output channel (1 to 8, then 1 again), as the SP-12's Set-up 17 does. A song's Mix step still sets the channels, as on the SP-12.
  • REC: choose sound files (several at once), or drop them on the catalog: WAV, AIFF, and MP3, FLAC, OGG as your browser reads them. Each becomes mono, 26,042 Hz, 12 bits with its peak at full level, and goes to the next empty pad, where the SP-12 would put a new sample; its end is cut when memory runs out. No room: no empty pad or no memory.
  • DOWNLOAD or DELETE (lamp lit; press again to leave): tap pads, then ENTER. DOWNLOAD saves WAV files (several: one .zip); DELETE asks first, then erases them as the SP-12 does.
  • Drag a pad onto another (touch: hold it first): the sound moves there; onto a sound, the two swap. The SEQ disk is not changed: segments play whatever sound number they hold (to change a pattern, use the segment editor).
  • Send the SOUND disk with Load Sounds (3 5).

V2.6 LOCK

In Set-up. It can be pressed while the sounds could live on stock v2.6 firmware: 5 s at most, in memory banks 6 / 7, on any pad. Pressed, the page moves the sounds into banks 6 / 7, so stock v2.6 loads the SOUND disk, and keeps it that way: REC fills only U1-U8 (stock's names for D1-D8) within 5 s; a pad can be dragged to any pad. Pressed again: back to 3.x (20 s); nothing moves back.

Editing

  • EDIT opens a segment's notes (rows A1 at the bottom up to D8 at the top, one bank at a time: scroll the box; RES the grid) or a song's steps. Brighter, longer bar = louder; +2 / -3 = tuning; amber number = decay (a sound in decay mode); white corner = off the grid. The small screen above the grid is the edit screen. The × at the panel's top right goes back; Escape does not close a panel.
  • Tap an empty step: a note for that row's sound on the RES grid (HiRes: that tick), with the level and tune of the note chosen last (none yet: a plain pad hit). Tap a note to choose it; again to let it go.
  • Edit screen: ← → move the underscore to a field, ENTER makes it flash, VALUE - / + change it (hold to repeat), ENTER again ends.
  • A note: Lvl 2-31; Tune -8..+7 (Decay for a sound in decay mode, Tu/Dc with no SOUND disk). Drag it to move it: left / right in RES steps (HiRes: ticks), up / down to another sound. Right-click it to delete it. Touch: drag a note to move it (a drag on the empty grid scrolls), hold it to delete it.
  • Several notes: drag a box on the empty grid (touch: hold, then drag), Shift + click a note (in or out), Shift + box (adds), Ctrl + A (all). The edit screen shows how many and the first one's values; VALUE changes them all. Drag one of them: they all move, or none (a step taken, or outside the segment). Delete, Backspace, a right-click or a long press on one of them deletes them all.
  • No note chosen (as EDIT opens; tap the bar numbers or the names, or a chosen note again): the segment's time signature, swing and length (bars, beats). A shorter segment asks before notes are cut.
  • A song: tap a step: its type, then its values. Types: segment, Begin Repeat, End Repeat, Mix, tempo at once, tempo over beats, Trigger, Link, Sub-song, Loop, End. A type given to the End goes in before it; End on an earlier step asks before the steps after it go. INSERT (before the step), DELETE. The list's first line: the song's tempo, its tenths and First Step. Tap a chosen segment step again: its notes.
  • Catalog: NEW in an empty number (on an empty SEQ disk it starts a new sequence file, as the SP-12 saves one after Clear Sequencer), COPY then tap an empty number, DELETE.
  • UNDO / REDO: every edit of either disk, until the page is closed.
  • Step taken: that sound has a note there. Memory full: the SP-12 could not load it.
  • An edit while it plays is heard from the next pass (a song: its next step).
  • Send the SEQ disk with Load Sequences (3 3).

3.2

The page always offers SP-12 Rambo 3.2 block numbers that no MIDI interface can damage. When the SP-12 accepts, the screen shows SP-12 Vers 3.2 / Safe mode on for a moment at the start of the transfer. Stock firmware and release 3.1 ignore the offer (no message); the page then repairs the block numbers that MIDI interfaces damage, as before.

Browsers

Chrome or Edge on Windows, Mac, Linux or Android. Safari and every browser on iPhone or iPad have no Web MIDI. On Android, open the page from its https address, not from a file. On Windows only one program can use a MIDI port at a time: close DAWs and other MIDI apps (the SP12 app too).

Log

LOG shows every MIDI message, IN from the SP-12 and OUT to it; COPY and DOWNLOAD give everything, with every byte. Its UNDO brings back a disk that a save or Format from the SP-12 replaced while its data were in no file; UNDO again swaps back.
